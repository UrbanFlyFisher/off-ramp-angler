# Privacy Policy — Off-Ramp Angler: Urban Fishing Guide

**Last updated: [fill in date]**

Off-Ramp Angler ("the app") is a reference guide to public and private fishing lakes in Los Angeles and Orange County, California. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

## What we collect

- **Usage analytics (optional, if enabled):** If analytics is turned on, we collect anonymous, aggregated usage data — which screens are viewed, which lakes are opened, and whether the "Get Directions" button is used. This is handled through Google Analytics and does not include your name, email, or exact location.
- **Location (only if you tap "Get Directions"):** Tapping this button hands off to Google Maps on your device. Off-Ramp Angler itself does not read, store, or transmit your device's location.
- **Crash/error reports (optional, if enabled):** If error monitoring is turned on, basic technical details about a crash (what happened, which screen) may be sent to help us fix bugs. This does not include personal information.

## What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise device location. We do not sell data to third parties. The app has no account/login system and no in-app chat or social features.

## Third-party services

- **Google Analytics** (if enabled) — governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Maps** — opened externally when you tap "Get Directions"; governed by Google's own privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

Off-Ramp Angler is a general-audience reference app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
